# Our Disney Trip planner

A phone-first planner for a Walt Disney World trip, built for my wife. She plans; I help. Keep it fun and cute, in the vintage Disneyland ticket-book colors.

## The trip
- Late September into early October 2027. Current trip dates: Sep 22 – Oct 5, 2027 (13 nights). Earlier option: Sep 19 – Oct 1 (12 nights).
- 2 adults + 3 kids (5 people). Total budget: **$15,000**.
- Booking a **DVC point rental**, ideally a 5th-sleeper Deluxe Studio (sleeps 5, kitchenette). A 1-bedroom costs about $3K more. A 2-bedroom (~$15K) is out.
- She wants: a kitchenette at least, walking distance to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios (extended evening hours there for deluxe/DVC guests), a Florida-beach or BoardWalk vibe, refillable resort mugs (Diet Coke, coffee).
- Front-runners: **BoardWalk Villas** studio (rooms around the pools near the boat launch) and **Old Key West** 1-bedroom (family nostalgia, cheapest 1BR, but no 5th-sleeper studio and a long walk for mug refills). Swan & Dolphin ruled out (no family suites, no kitchenette).
- Later dates are cooler but cost more; earlier are cheaper but hotter.

## The app
- **Live at https://dtp.ondorilabo.com.** A Cloudflare Worker (`worker/index.js`, config `wrangler.jsonc`) serves `index.html` and keeps the shared plan in the D1 database `disney-trip-planner` (table `docs`). The README has the API and the deploy command.
- **Accounts** (website only): sign in with a 6-digit code emailed through Resend (`RESEND_API_KEY` Worker secret; ondorilabo.com is verified in Resend; sender `trips@ondorilabo.com`). Without the secret (local `wrangler dev`) the code goes to the log. Session = HttpOnly cookie `dtp_session`. Tables in `worker/migrations/0002_accounts.sql` and `0003_friend_codes.sql`: `users` (with `code`, the friend code), `codes` (sign-in codes), `sessions`, `friends` (both directions), `members` (trip, user, owner), `friend_tries`. Friends are made with a 6-character friend code (letters and digits without 0/O/1/I/L). The **Friends tab** (icon `mickey-minnie`) shows your code with Copy, an "Add a friend" box, and each friend with how you know them (`friends.rel`, set by you, only you see it; `PUT /api/friends/<id>`) and your trips together (trips you're on that they started or are linked on). Linking a Who's going row to a friend fills in their name and that relationship. The account pop-up is just your name and Sign out. 10 wrong codes an hour per account; "Get a new friend code" replaces yours). A trip is seen and edited by its members: the creator (owner, the only one who can delete it) and the accounts linked on Who's going (`people[].uid`, friends only; the Worker drops other links). The Worker filters `GET /api/docs` to your trips plus the shared `dvc`/`ref`/`news`, and refuses writes to trips you're not on. Brittany's and Dan's account ids are `brittany` and `dan`, matching the old hearts/pros-and-cons ids (`tools/seed-accounts.sql`). A brand-new account gets a first trip with them on it. Writes need the header `x-dtp: 1`.
- One self-contained page: `index.html` (HTML/CSS/JS, no build step). Tabs (`TABS`): Home, Resorts, Compare (tab key `dates`), Plan, Budget, Dreams, Food, To-do, News, My Trips, Friends (website only, needs an account; `tabOn`). The bar shows 5 plus a "More" button, like Teams; More has a Reorder list (Home stays first), saved per phone in localStorage (`our-disney-trip-v1:nav`). News is also a card on Home (newest 3, stories that mention one of our resorts first, matched by `RESORT_WORDS`) with the full list in a pop-up.
- Plan tab: one card per day she adds ("Add Day N"); no dates, by choice, since the trip length isn't settled. "Reorder days" opens a compact pop-up list (`showDayOrder`) with drag handles like the Teams menu; Done rewrites days/d1…dN in the new order (no dragging the big cards, by choice: busy days are too tall). Deleting a day moves later days up (no gaps). Each day is a park from `DAY_PARKS` (incl. Universal) or a Rest/Travel day, with a schedule of items by time. Park art is `PARK_ART` (Universal = globe, Rest day = canopy boat, Travel day = vintage car).
- Resorts is where she enters scenarios by hand (resort, dates, the price she found, a 1–5 star rating, perks and amenities tags); real prices can't be synced. Each gets points (`PTS` and `scoreOf` in `index.html`), with a receipt in a pop-up when you tap the score: +12 per star, +3 per night (up to 14), +1 per $100 under the resort budget (max +10) or −2 per $100 over, −3 per degree the usual high averages above 82°, +3 per perk (up to 4), +1 per amenity (up to 6), ±1 per pro/con (net ±5). Unrated options get no points. No user weights, by choice. Rainy season (NWS, May 27 – Oct 15) and hurricane season (NOAA, Jun 1 – Nov 30) are shown as icons, not scored. Compare ranks by points and shows up to 3 side by side.
- Storage modes: on the website it uses the Worker API and checks for changes every 5 seconds. The claude.ai artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/XoojCoRWJGJiB31w4jiKoQ) still runs but is **no longer the live copy**. Opened as a file, it uses localStorage seeded from `STARTER`.
- Change live data with `npx wrangler@4 d1 execute disney-trip-planner --remote` (the API now needs a signed-in session), not with the artifact tools.
- Ticket prices: 4 adult-price + 1 child ticket (Amelia, 13, and Avery, 10, pay adult prices by the trip; Annie is 3). The ticket estimator card is gone (`ref/tickets` is still in the db, unused); she sets the tickets amount herself.
- **My Trips** (tab key `trips`, suitcases icon `luggage`): several trips, each with its own name, people and plan. `trips/<id>`: `{name, people: [{name, rel}], created}` (rel = relationship to her, free text with `RELATIONS` suggestions). Home shows a head count ("5 going"), **no adult/kid split or ages, by choice**: Disney tickets count 10+ as adults but resorts 18+, so it would mislead. Older people entries may still carry an `age`, ignored. The old `adults`/`kids` settings are only a fallback head count when a trip has no people list. The first trip is `main` and uses the plain collection names; later trips (ids like `t<base36 time>`) use `<coll>@<id>` for `TRIP_COLLS` (trip, resorts, budget, wishes, food, todos, proscons, days). `dvc`, `ref`, `news` are shared. Each phone remembers its open trip (`our-disney-trip-v1:trip`). A new trip starts with the open trip's people and opens "Who's going". Edit (`editTrip(id)`) works on any trip, open or not; delete is in that sheet. The first trip and the open one can't be deleted.
- Collections (one doc per item):
  - `trip/settings`: `{budget, start, end, adults, kids, pickId, tixDays, tixAdults, tixKids}` (an old `scoreW` may still be there, unused); `trip/compare`: `{ids: [resort ids, up to 3]}`
  - `proscons/<resort-slug>`: `{name, pros[], cons[]}` (items `{id, text, by}`)
  - `dvc/<resort>` (official 2027 points charts), `ref/seasons2027`, `ref/weather`, `ref/tickets`, `ref/news`, `news/<id>`
  - `resorts/<id>`: `{name, room, start, end, total, desire (0–5 stars), perks[], amenities[], vibe, notes, hearts[], out} (perks and amenities are free tags; older docs still have size/sleeps/kitchen/walk/eeh, which show as tags until the option is edited and saved; an old `source` is kept but no longer shown or edited)`
  - `budget/<id>`: `{label, cat, amount, note, status?}`, one doc per budget item she adds (older ones use the category as the id). `cat` (stay, tickets, travel, food, mugs, fun, other) only sets the color and the meter group; a category can hold several items. Amounts edit in place; tapping a name opens rename/move/delete; `status: "Estimate"` shows an Estimate pill. With no stay item, a stand-in resort row uses the picked resort's price (typing there saves `budget/stay`); stay items are what resort prices are scored against.
  - `wishes/<id>`: `{text, park, hearts[], done}`
  - `food/<id>`: `{text, place, meal, note, hearts[], done}`. `place` is one of `FOOD_PLACES` (the parks, Universal, Disney Springs, "Resort meal", "Room meal"); `meal` is "" or breakfast|lunch|dinner|snack. Food tab art: pretzel; Resort meal = pot pie, Room meal = spaghetti.
  - `todos/<id>`: `{text, months|days|due, done, note}` (dates count back from check-in)
  - `days/d<N>` (Day N of the trip, not tied to a date): `{park, note, items: [{id, time "HH:MM" or "", text, kind (ride|food|show|shop|rest|other)}]}`
- The db is already seeded with her real quotes (from her screenshots of a DVC rental site). The budget lines for tickets, travel, food, mugs and souvenirs are **my guesses**, marked "Estimate".

## Conventions
- Work on branch `claude/disney-vacation-planner-341o6o`.
- Keep `index.html` single-file. Colors are tokens in `:root` with dark-mode overrides; keep the vintage Disneyland ticket-book palette (castle red #C9472D, navy #0D1C2E, mint #95D6B3, brown stub #905318, cream #FFF1CB, orange #FF9E44, peach #F4D0B3, yellow #FFC754, sage #C5D49F) and the Fredoka/Nunito fonts.
