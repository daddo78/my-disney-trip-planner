# Our Disney Trip

A pink, phone-friendly planner for our Walt Disney World trip (late September 2027, 2 adults + 3 kids, $15,000 budget).

- **Home**: countdown to check-in, budget at a glance, our resort pick, what's coming up.
- **Resorts**: compare DVC rental quotes and other options (price, per night, points, kitchen, walk to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios, extended evening hours), heart favorites, and make one "our pick".
- **Budget**: the picked resort fills in the stay line; add tickets, travel, food, mugs and souvenirs and see what's left of the $15K.
- **Dream list**: rides, snacks and moments by park.
- **To-do**: booking windows and reminders, dated by counting back from check-in.

It's one file, `index.html`. Open it in a browser and it saves on that device. Published as a claude.ai artifact, it saves to shared storage so both of us see the same plan.
